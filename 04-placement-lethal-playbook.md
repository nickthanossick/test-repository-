# 04 — Lethal Placement Machine (Irreplaceable Banne ka System)

> **Context:** 6-member team. Data aapke control mein. 230+ offers aapke, highest + big companies aapke.
> Ab chhoti companies chhod ke **big companies** pe focus. Goal: itne high-value ban jao ki aapke bina
> team ka system hi na chale.
>
> **Ethical guardrail (padho):** "Lethal" = *itni value create karo ki log aap pe naturally depend karein* —
> **best builder + owner** ban ke. NOT teammates ko sabotage karke ya data chhupa ke unhe fail karana.
> Wo short-term power deta hai, long-term reputation todta hai. Real, durable leverage sirf
> **competence + systems + results** se banti hai. Ye playbook usi raaste ka hai.

## 1. Own The System — data ko team ka "nervous system" bana do
*(Model: Thinking in Systems — leverage points; The Pyramid Principle — structure)*

- Aap data control karte ho — isko random spreadsheet se **single source of truth** mein upgrade karo:
  - **Master pipeline tracker:** har student/candidate × har company × stage (applied → screen → rounds → offer → closed).
  - **Company database:** har company ka JD pattern, rounds, cutoff, HR contact, past questions.
  - **Live dashboard view:** offers count, conversion %, big-company pipeline — ek nazar mein.
- Jab team ka har faisla *tumhare* system se hota hai, to system = tum. Yahi structural leverage hai.
- **Rule:** system ko *documented + clean* rakho — chhupao mat. Jo cheez sab dekhte hain par sirf tum *chala* sakte ho, wahi asली moat hai.

## 2. Big-Company Playbook — repeatable, na ki lucky
*(Model: Bulletproof Problem Solving — 7 steps; McKinsey — hypothesis-driven)*

Big companies chhoti se alag khelti hain. Ek **repeatable pipeline** banao:
1. **Target list (MECE):** tier-1 companies ko segment karo (product / service / startup-unicorn / MNC).
2. **Research:** har company ka hiring pattern, JD keywords, interview rounds, past questions (apna DB banao).
3. **Referral map:** alumni + LinkedIn + past-placed students = warm intro > cold apply. Referral rate track karo.
4. **Tailored outreach:** generic nahi — har company ke liye customized resume + message (Pyramid Principle: top-down, crisp).
5. **Interview-prep pipeline:** company-specific mock rounds, question banks, feedback loop.
6. **Close:** offer aane pe negotiation (neeche #4).

> Metric: sirf offer count nahi — **big-company conversion %** track karo. Yahi tumhe baaki 5 se alag karta hai.

## 3. Institutional Knowledge = Moat
*(Model: So Good They Can't Ignore You — career capital)*

- HR contacts, company relationships, process playbooks, "kya kaam karta hai" — sab ek jagah document karo.
- Ye knowledge tum *build + run* karte ho, isliye value tumhare saath chalti hai.
- **Career capital** jitna rare + valuable, utni leverage. Roz thoda add karo — 30 din mein bada asset.

## 4. Negotiation & Closing Leverage
*(Model: Never Split the Difference — Chris Voss)*

- **Mirror + Label:** "Lagta hai timeline ko lekar concern hai..." — samne wale ko samjha hua feel karao.
- **Calibrated questions:** "Hum isse kaise possible bana sakte hain?" — control tumhare paas.
- **No-oriented Qs:** "Kya ye idea galat hoga agar...?" — log "no" mein safe feel karte hain.
- Higher packages close karoge → tumhari "highest placement" wali position aur strong.

## 5. Force Multiplier — team ka operating system bano
*(Model: The Effective Executive — Drucker; 7 Habits — proactive)*

- Apni process ko **template + checklist** bana ke team ko do. Log tumhare system pe productive honge → credit + dependency dono tumhare.
- "Sirf main kar sakta hoon" wale kaam pehchano, aur unko *repeatable system* mein convert karo (taaki scale ho, par ownership tumhari rahe).
- Doosron ko upar uthana = tum leader ban jaate ho, sirf contributor nahi. **Yahi asली "lethal" hai** — replaceable contributor nahi, irreplaceable architect.

## 6. Metrics Moat — apni story numbers mein
*(Model: Superforecasting — track record; Signal & Noise — signal vs noise)*

- Ek **scorecard** rakho: total offers (230+), big-company offers, highest package, conversion %, avg turnaround.
- Har hafte update. Ye numbers = tumhara undeniable proof. Argument nahi, evidence.
- Jab data bolega, position khud-ba-khud secure hogi.
