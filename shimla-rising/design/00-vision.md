# Vision

## Ek line mein

Shimla ki dhalanon pe ek mechanic, jo apne baap ki jali hui garage se shuru karke
sheher ke timber-aur-cement wale nexus tak pahunchta hai.

## Kyun Shimla

GTA-type games aksar ek kaalpanik badeh sheher gadhte hain — flat, grid, chaudi
sadkein. Shimla uska ulta hai, aur wahi isse interesting banata hai:

- **Sheher ek ridge par hai.** Do gali ke beech 40 m ki oonchai ka farak ho sakta
  hai. "Seedha jao" ka matlab yahan hai nahi.
- **Mall Road pe gaadi ban hai.** Sheher ka sabse mashhoor hissa drivable hi
  nahi — jo ek open-world game ke liye ek asli, dilchasp constraint hai.
- **Mausam gameplay hai.** Dec–Feb barf, Jul–Sep monsoon aur landslide. Saal ka
  aadha hissa sadak khud dushman hoti hai.
- **Ek narrow-gauge toy train** sheher ke beech se guzarti hai — 762 mm gauge,
  102 tunnel, UNESCO World Heritage.

Ek pahadi sheher ki geography khud level design hai. Usse gadhna nahi pada,
sirf naapna pada.

## Kahani ka core

Ye "gareeb ladka bada gangster banta hai" wali kahani nahi hai. Vicky kabhi
sheher ka maalik nahi banta. Wo bas ye samajhna chahta hai ki uske baap ka naam
us register mein kyun tha — aur jab samajh aata hai, to use tay karna padta hai
ki us sach ka kya karna hai.

Antagonist bhi ek aadmi nahi hai. Rana Sahab ek intezaam ka chehra hai: zameen,
timber, tender, aur wo raftaar jisse ek hill station apni hi dhalan pe zarurat se
zyada bhaari ho jaata hai. Isiliye khatam hone par Rana haarta hai, par sheher
badalta nahi — sirf kahani chhap jaati hai.

## Design principles

1. **Har mechanic Shimla se aaye.** Agar koi feature kisi bhi sheher mein fit
   ho jaata hai, to shayad usse kaatna chahiye.
2. **Naksha asli rahe.** Landmark asli jagah pe, asli oonchai pe. Khiladi jo
   Shimla gaya ho, use pehchan aani chahiye.
3. **Ek data layer.** Kahani, naksha, missions — sab text data. Do engines usse
   padhte hain. Content banane wale ko code chhune ki zaroorat na ho.
4. **Kisi cheez ka dikhawa nahi.** Jo abhi kaam nahi karta, wo docs mein saaf
   likha ho.
