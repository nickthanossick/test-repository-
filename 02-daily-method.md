# 02 — Daily Reading Method (Padhne ka System)

> Book *padhna* aasaan hai; usko *use* karna mushkil. Ye method ensure karta hai ki har din ki book tumhare
> thinking mein permanently install ho — sirf pages palatna na ho.

---

## 🕒 Din ka structure (total reading ~3–4 hrs intense)

### Step 0 — Setup (2 min)
- Phone silent + doosre room mein. Deep Work mode (Day 27 wali book ka pura point yahi hai).
- `books/day-XX.md` open rakho — saath-saath bharoge.

### Step 1 — Pre-read / Recon (10 min)
- Title, subtitle, back cover, contents padho.
- Intro + conclusion pehle padho (spoiler chahiye — destination pata ho to raasta samajh aata hai).
- **1 sawaal likho:** *"Ye book kaunsa problem solve karti hai, aur main isse kya chahta hoon?"*

### Step 2 — Active Read: 3-Pass Method
- **Pass 1 (Structure):** har chapter ke headings + first/last paragraph. Poora skeleton dimaag mein.
- **Pass 2 (Argument):** har chapter ka **main claim + evidence** pakdo. Margin mein / notes mein 1-line summary.
- **Pass 3 (Detail):** sirf *important* sections deep. Boring/known parts skim. (Sab kuch equal weight nahi.)
- ⚠️ Long books: Pass 1 + 2 poori, Pass 3 sirf key chapters pe.

### Step 3 — Capture (20 min) — **sabse zaroori step**
`books/day-XX.md` mein bharo:
- **3 Key Models/Ideas** — apne shabdon mein (copy-paste nahi).
- **3 Takeaways** — "isse mera thinking kaise badla?"
- **1 Placement Application** — *"is idea ko main apne placement/team system mein kaise use karun?"* (ye har din ka secret weapon hai).
- **1 Quote** jo yaad rakhni hai.

### Step 4 — Nightly Add (5 min)
- Din ka **best 1 model** → `03-mental-models-deck.md` mein add karo (naam + 1-line + kab use karna).
- `05-progress-tracker.md` mein aaj ka box tick karo.

---

## 🧠 Retention boosters (thoda extra, bada fayda)

- **Feynman test:** book ka core idea kisi 12-saal ke bacche ko samjha ke dekho (khud ko bol ke). Atak gaye = wahan gap hai.
- **Spaced review:** har Sunday pichhle hafte ke 7 `day-XX.md` files 10 min mein skim karo.
- **Connect, don't collect:** naya model purane models se jodo — "ye kis se milta hai? kahan clash karta hai?" Isolated facts bhool jaate hain; connected network yaad rehta hai.
- **Teach it:** kisi teammate ko din ka 1 idea 2 min mein sunao. Teaching = strongest encoding (+ tum smart dikhte ho 😉).

---

## ⚡ Agar time kam pade (busy placement din)

Minimum viable din:
1. Intro + conclusion + 2 key chapters (60–90 min).
2. `books/day-XX.md` mein sirf **3 key models + 1 placement application** bharo.
3. 1 model deck mein add karo.

Itna bhi kiya to din "count" hota hai. **Perfection se zyada consistency.**
