## 🌱 Starter models (Day 0 — pehle se seeded, taaki template clear ho)

### First Principles — (Starter)
- **Kya hai:** Cheez ko uske basic truths tak todo, phir wahan se naya banao — analogy/copy se nahi.
- **Kab use karna:** Jab "aisa hi hota hai" sun-sun ke stuck ho.
- **Placement example:** "Big companies se offer *kaise* banta hai?" — raw process todo (JD fit → referral → screen → rounds → close), har step optimize karo, na ki "jo sab karte hain" copy karo.

### Inversion — (Starter)
- **Kya hai:** "Success kaise?" ke bajaye "Ye *fail* kaise hoga?" pucho, phir un cheezon se bacho.
- **Kab use karna:** Planning, risk, koi important decision.
- **Placement example:** "Meri team ka structure kaise toot sakta hai / offers kaise ruk sakte hain?" — un failure points ko main hi own kar loon.

### Second-Order Thinking — (Starter)
- **Kya hai:** "Aur phir kya?" — immediate result ke baad ke 2-3 effects socho.
- **Kab use karna:** Har decision jiska ripple effect ho.
- **Placement example:** Chhoti companies chhod ke big pe focus — first-order: kam offers. Second-order: brand + quality + leverage badhta hai. Third-order: team ka positioning upgrade.
