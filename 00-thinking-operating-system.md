## 🧨 Problem: React vs Respond

Zyadatar log **react** karte hain — trigger aaya, turant emotion, turant action. Ye System-1 (fast, auto) hai.
Structured thinker **respond** karta hai — trigger aur action ke beech ek **gap** banata hai, us gap mein process chalata hai.

> Viktor Frankl: *"Stimulus aur response ke beech ek space hota hai. Us space mein hamari power hai — apna response choose karne ki."*

**Poora game = us gap ko bada karna, aur us gap mein ek repeatable process daalna.**

---

## 🔁 The Core Loop — STRIDES (7 steps)

Koi bhi problem, decision, ya reaction aaye — isko chalao:

### 1. STOP (Pause)
- 10-second rule: react karne se pehle ruk jao.
- Apni emotion ko **naam do**: "Main abhi defensive/anxious/excited feel kar raha hoon." Naam dete hi uski power kam ho jaati hai.
- Sawaal: *"Kya ye abhi decide karna zaroori hai, ya main sooch sakta hoon?"*

### 2. FRAME (Asli sawaal pakdo)
- **First-principles:** "Yahan asli problem/decision kya hai?" Assumptions ko alag karo facts se.
- Ek line mein likho: *"Mujhe ye decide karna hai: ______"*
- Galat frame = galat answer. 80% galtiyaan yahin hoti hain.

### 3. STRUCTURE (Todo — MECE)
- Problem ko **MECE** buckets mein todo (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive — overlap nahi, gap nahi).
- **Issue tree** banao: main question → 3-4 sub-questions → unke drivers.
- Ek complex cheez → chhote answerable tukde.

### 4. REASON (Sochne ke modes)
- **Deductive:** rule → case → conclusion (pakka).
- **Inductive:** examples → pattern (probable).
- **Abductive:** best explanation for evidence (detective wala).
- **Base rate** check: "Aam taur pe aisi cheezein kaise nikalti hain?" (representativeness bias se bacho).
- **Second-order:** "Aur phir kya? Uske baad kya?" (2-3 steps aage socho).

### 5. DECIDE (Choose)
- **Weighted criteria:** 3-4 factors likho, weight do, options ko score do.
- **Expected value:** probability × outcome. Feelings se nahi, EV se decide karo.
- **1-way vs 2-way door (Bezos):** reversible decision? → fast decide. Irreversible? → slow, zyada data.

### 6. EXAMINE (Stress-test)
- **Inversion:** "Isko *fail* kaise karaun?" Ulta socho, phir un cheezon se bacho.
- **Pre-mortem:** "6 mahine baad ye plan fail ho gaya — kyun?" Ab likho.
- **Red-team:** apne hi decision ke **against** sabse strong argument banao. Survive kare to strong hai.
- **Steelman:** dusre ke view ka sabse strong version banao, phir uska jawab do.

### 7. SET & REVIEW (Act + Learn)
- Decision **journal** mein likho: kya decide kiya, kyun, kya expect karte ho.
- Outcome aane pe wapas dekho: **decision achha tha ya sirf outcome achha tha?** (process vs luck — *Thinking in Bets*).
- Feedback loop = yahin se real learning aati hai.

## ⏱️ Daily Rituals (5-10 min total)

- **Morning Framing (3 min):** "Aaj ka 1 sabse important decision/question kya hai?" Usko frame karo.
- **Decision Journal (as needed):** koi bhi non-trivial decision → 3 lines: decision, reasoning, expected outcome.
- **Evening Review (5 min):** "Aaj kahan react kiya jab respond karna chahiye tha? Kal kya better?"
- **Weekly Synthesis (Sunday, 20 min):** hafte ke naye models ko connect karo, decision journal review karo.

## 🧰 The 12 Always-On Mental Models (ratta maar lo)

1. **First principles** — assumptions todo, base se socho.
2. **MECE** — no overlap, no gap.
3. **Second-order thinking** — "aur phir kya?"
4. **Inversion** — problem ko ulta karke socho.
5. **Base rates** — pehle average dekho, phir specifics.
6. **Expected value** — probability × payoff.
7. **Opportunity cost** — is choice se main kya *chhod* raha hoon?
8. **Circle of competence** — main kya *actually* jaanta hoon?
9. **Map ≠ territory** — model reality nahi hai; assumptions check karo.
10. **Occam's razor** — simplest explanation usually best.
11. **Confirmation bias** — main sirf apne view ka proof to nahi dhoondh raha?
12. **Margin of safety** — buffer rakho; sab kuch plan ke hisaab se nahi hota.

> Ye 12 = tumhara har-din arsenal. Baaki 30 books inko deep aur wide karengi.

## ✅ Ek Practical Drill (roz 1 baar)

Din mein **ek** real situation lo (koi placement decision, koi argument, koi choice) aur **STRIDES** poora likh ke chalao — 10 min. 30 din baad ye automatic ho jayega. Yahi "brain reset" hai.
