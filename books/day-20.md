# Day 20 — Superforecasting — Philip Tetlock & Dan Gardner
**Phase 4: Decisions & probability** | **~290 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — traits + method pe focus.

## Kyun padhni hai
Kuch log dusron se consistently behtar predict karte hain — kyun? Ye book unki *aadatein* sikhati hai. Placement = prediction ka khel (kaun convert karega, kaunsi company hire karegi).

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Outside view first (base rates)** — pehle general average, phir specifics adjust.
- **Fermi-ize** — bade sawaal ko chhote answerable tukdon mein todo.
- **Update in small steps** — naya evidence aane pe belief thoda-thoda badlo (Bayesian).
- **Keep score (Brier score)** — apni predictions track karo, honestly.
- **Dragonfly eye** — kai perspectives ko merge karo.
- Superforecasters: humble, numerate, growth-minded, non-ideological.

## Placement application
> Har candidate ke liye ek **probability** do ("70% is company mein convert hoga"), aur outcome ke baad **score** karo — 30 din mein tumhara judgment calibrate ho jayega. **Base rate:** "is college se is company mein historically kितne% jaate hain" — wahan se start karo, gut se nahi.
