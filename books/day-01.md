# Day 1 — The Art of Thinking Clearly — Rolf Dobelli
**Phase 1: Bias & mind** | **~340 pages** | **Read strategy:** Easy — 99 chhote chapters, har ek 1 bias. 3-4 min per chapter.

## Kyun padhni hai
Start ke liye perfect — apne dimaag ke sabse common "bugs" ka ek quick catalog. Baaki poori journey ke liye foundation.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Survivorship bias** — sirf "jeetne walon" ko dekhte hain, fail hue nahi dikhte → galat conclusion.
- **Sunk cost fallacy** — pehle laga time/paisa waste ho gaya to log aur invest karte hain. Wo gaya, ab aage dekho.
- **Confirmation bias** — sirf apne view ka proof dhoondhna.
- **Social proof** — "sab kar rahe hain" isliye sahi maan lena.
- **Availability bias** — jo yaad aa jaata hai usko zyada likely samajhna.

## Placement application
> **Survivorship bias:** "Jo students place ho gaye" ke saath-saath "jo nahi hue aur kyun" ka bhi data rakho — asli pattern wahin hai. Aur **sunk cost:** kisi chhoti-company pipeline pe already time laga diya isliye usko na chhodna — galat; big-company shift ke liye cut karo.
