# Day 4 — Predictably Irrational — Dan Ariely
**Phase 1: Bias & mind** | **~280 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — story-driven, fast.

## Kyun padhni hai
Hum "randomly" galti nahi karte — **systematically, predictably** karte hain. Ye patterns jaan lo to inhe apne aur doosron ke faisle mein pehchaan loge (aur use bhi kar sakoge).

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Relativity / Decoy effect** — 3rd "trap" option daal do to log beech wala choose karte hain.
- **Anchoring** — pehla number/impression baaki sab ko color kar deta hai.
- **The cost of zero ("FREE")** — "free" logic ko off kar deta hai.
- **Social vs market norms** — paisa laate hi relationship dynamics badal jaate hain.
- **Endowment effect** — jo hamara hai usko over-value karte hain.

## Placement application
> **Anchoring** negotiation mein huge hai — pehla number set karne wala control mein hota hai (Day 29 se link). **Decoy/relativity:** candidates ko offers present karte waqt framing matter karti hai. **Social norms:** team goodwill = market norms se zyada powerful; relationships transactionally mat todo.
