# Day 9 — Asking the Right Questions: A Guide to Critical Thinking — Browne & Keeley
**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~200 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — practical, checklist-style.

## Kyun padhni hai
Critical thinking = achhe **sawaal** poochna. Ye book ek repeatable question-checklist deti hai jise kisi bhi claim/report/pitch pe chala sakte ho.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Issue & conclusion kya hai?** — pehle ye pin karo.
- **Reasons kya hain?** — conclusion ko support karne wale.
- **Ambiguous words** — "success", "best" — inko define karao.
- **Assumptions (value + descriptive)** — jo bina bole maan liya gaya.
- **Evidence ki quality** + **rival causes** + **stats se dhokha** (misleading numbers).

## Placement application
> Har vendor/company claim, har team proposal pe ye checklist chalao: "conclusion kya? reasons kya? kaunsa assumption chhupa hai? numbers honest hain?" Ye tumhe woh insaan banata hai jo BS turant pakad leta hai — meetings mein sabse sharp.
