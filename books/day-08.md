# Day 8 — A Rulebook for Arguments — Anthony Weston
**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~130 pages** | **Read strategy:** Very short reference — padho + bookmark.

## Kyun padhni hai
Argument "jeetna" nahi — *sahi* argument banana. Ye chhoti si book har type ke argument ka rulebook hai. Lifelong reference.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Premises → conclusion** — har argument ka skeleton clearly identify karo.
- **Arguments by example / analogy / authority / cause** — har ek ke apne rules.
- **Deductive forms** — modus ponens (agar P to Q; P; isliye Q), modus tollens.
- **Correlation ≠ causation** — sabse common galti.
- **Reliable sources** + rival explanations ko address karo.

## Placement application
> Apni recommendations ko **argument structure** de ke present karo: claim + 2-3 solid reasons + evidence (numbers). Seniors/companies ke sasamne tum data-backed, structured dikhoge — emotional nahi. "Correlation ≠ causation": "is strategy se offers badhe" — pakka strategy hi wajah thi? verify.
