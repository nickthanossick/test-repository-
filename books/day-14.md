# Day 14 — The Pyramid Principle — Barbara Minto
**Phase 3: Problem solving** | **~330 pages** | **Read strategy:** Part 1 (structure) MUST; Part 2-4 selective.

## Kyun padhni hai
McKinsey ki legendary structured-thinking + communication bible. Ideas ko **top-down pyramid** mein organize karna — taaki tum clearly socho aur crisply bolo. Tumhari "structured" image ka core.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Pyramid structure** — pehle answer (top), phir supporting arguments, phir data. Top-down.
- **SCQA** — Situation → Complication → Question → Answer (intro banane ka formula).
- **MECE grouping** — supporting points overlap na karein, gap na chhode.
- **Governing thought** — har group ka ek summary statement.
- **Inductive vs deductive** grouping of arguments.

## Placement application
> Har report/pitch/update ko **pyramid** mein do: pehle bottom-line ("is quarter big-company offers 40% badhe"), phir 3 MECE reasons, phir data. Seniors/companies ke saath tum sabse clear communicator banoge — aur clarity = authority.
