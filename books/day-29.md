# Day 29 — Never Split the Difference — Chris Voss
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~290 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — tactics highlight karo, roleplay in head.

## Kyun padhni hai
Ex-FBI hostage negotiator ki playbook. Negotiation = daily leverage: offers close karna, packages negotiate, stakeholders align karna. Direct placement power.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Tactical empathy** — samne wale ki feelings samjho + naam do.
- **Mirroring** — unke aakhri 1-3 shabd repeat → wo aur khulte hain.
- **Labeling** — "Aisa lagta hai ki aap ___ ko lekar concerned hain" → emotion defuse.
- **Calibrated questions** — "How / What" wale open Qs ("Hum isse kaise kaam bana sakte hain?") → control tumhare paas.
- **"No" is good; "That's right" > "Yes"** — real agreement ke signals. **Accusation audit** — pehle hi worst objections bol do.

## Placement application
> Offer negotiation, HR se timelines, candidate counseling — sab mein **labeling + calibrated Qs** use karo. "How am I supposed to do that?" politely bol ke better terms. Ye skill directly tumhari "highest package" wali position ko aur strong karti hai.
