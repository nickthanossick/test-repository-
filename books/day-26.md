# Day 26 — The Effective Executive — Peter Drucker
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~180 pages** | **Read strategy:** Short — slow, har chapter ek principle.

## Kyun padhni hai
Management ke baap ki timeless book: effectiveness *seekhi* jaa sakti hai. Talent nahi, *habits* effective banati hain. Tumhare "machine" banne ka blueprint.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Know thy time** — pehle apna time record karo, phir manage. Time hi sabse scarce resource.
- **Focus on contribution** — "main kya *result* de sakta hoon?" (activity nahi, outcome).
- **Build on strengths** — apni + team ki strengths pe khelo, weakness fix karne mein time barbaad mat karo.
- **First things first** — concentration; ek waqt mein ek badi cheez.
- **Effective decisions** — few but fundamental; boundary conditions clear.

## Placement application
> **Focus on contribution:** "mera unique contribution kya hai jo koi aur nahi de sakta?" — usko double down karo. **Build on strengths:** team ke 6 log — har ek ki strength pehchano, wahan lagao; tum orchestrator bano. **Concentration:** ek waqt mein ek bada lever (abhi: big-company system).
