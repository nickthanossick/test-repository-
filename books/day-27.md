# Day 27 — Deep Work — Cal Newport
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~280 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — Part 1 (why) skim, Part 2 (rules) deep.

## Kyun padhni hai
Distracted duniya mein **focus** hi rare superpower hai. Ye book batati hai kaise deep, high-value kaam kiya jaaye — jahan asli leverage banti hai (aur jahan ye 30-day reading bhi possible hoti hai).

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Deep vs Shallow work** — deep = cognitively demanding, value-creating, hard to replicate. Shallow = logistical, replaceable.
- **Attention residue** — task-switching se focus "bacha hua" pichhle task pe atka rehta hai.
- **Deliberate practice** — focused effort at the edge of ability = skill growth.
- **Rituals & routines** — deep work ko schedule + environment se protect karo.
- **Drain the shallows** — shallow kaam minimize/batch/delegate.

## Placement application
> Jo kaam sirf tum kar sakte ho (system design, strategy, big-company relationships) = **deep work** — usko protected time do. Routine follow-ups = **shallow** — template/system/delegate. Ye distinction hi tumhe replaceable contributor se irreplaceable architect mein badalti hai.
