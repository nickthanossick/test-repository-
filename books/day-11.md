# Day 11 — Logic: A Very Short Introduction — Graham Priest
**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~130 pages** | **Read strategy:** Short but dense — slow padho, examples khud solve karo.

## Kyun padhni hai
Formal logic ki neenv — validity actually kya hoti hai. Thoda academic, par ye tumhari reasoning ko "precise" banata hai.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Validity** — agar premises sach hain to conclusion *zaroor* sach ho (form ki baat).
- **Deductive vs inductive** — certainty vs probability.
- **Conditionals (if–then)** — "if P then Q" ka asli matlab; common galtiyan (affirming the consequent).
- **Quantifiers** — "all", "some", "none" — inko precisely handle karna.
- **Self-reference & paradoxes** — logic ki limits.

## Placement application
> **Conditionals** roz kaam aate hain: "Agar referral hai to interview milega" — ulta sach nahi (interview mila matlab referral tha, ye galat). In logical traps se bacho jab team strategy plan karti hai. Precise "if-then" thinking = fewer planning errors.
