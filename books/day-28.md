# Day 28 — Good Strategy Bad Strategy — Richard Rumelt
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~300 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — "kernel" concept pe focus.

## Kyun padhni hai
"Strategy" word sabse zyada misuse hota hai. Rumelt sikhata hai asli strategy kaisi dikhti hai — goals ki list nahi, balki ek sharp diagnosis + action. Tumhare placement plan ko *real* strategy banayegi.

## Key ideas — The Kernel (dhyaan idhar)
- **1. Diagnosis** — asli challenge kya hai (simplify the mess).
- **2. Guiding policy** — challenge se nipatne ka overall approach.
- **3. Coherent action** — aapas mein juda hua concrete steps.
- **Bad strategy signs:** fluff, problem ko face na karna, goals ko strategy samajhna, conflicting objectives.
- **Leverage & proximate objectives** — ek achievable nearby target pe force concentrate karo.

## Placement application
> Apne big-company push ko **kernel** do: *Diagnosis* ("top offers ke liye referral + prep gap hai") → *Guiding policy* ("warm-intro + company-specific prep pe focus") → *Coherent actions* (alumni map, mock rounds, DB). "Zyada offers laao" = goal, strategy nahi. Ye difference tumhe strategist banata hai.
