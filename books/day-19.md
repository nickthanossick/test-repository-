# Day 19 — How to Decide — Annie Duke
**Phase 4: Decisions & probability** | **~250 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — workbook style, exercises karo.

## Kyun padhni hai
Phase 4 shuru. Day 2 (Thinking in Bets) ka **practical toolkit** version — actual decisions kaise process karein, step by step.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Decision multiverse** — sirf jo hua nahi, jo ho *sakta* tha — sab possibilities map karo.
- **Preferences, Payoffs, Probabilities (3 Ps)** — har option ke liye.
- **Backcasting & pre-mortem** — future se ulta socho (success + failure dono).
- **Only-option test** — "agar ye ekmaatr option hota, tab bhi choose karta?"
- **Decision hygiene** — dusron ki opinion se pehle apni likho (anchoring se bachne ko).

## Placement application
> Bade calls (kaunsi companies target karein) pe **3 Ps** likho + **pre-mortem** karo ("ye plan fail hua to kyun?"). **Decision hygiene:** team se raay lene se pehle sab apna estimate alag likhein — groupthink khatam, better decisions.
