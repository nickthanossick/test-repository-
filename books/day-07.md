# Day 7 — Being Logical — D.Q. McInerny
**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~150 pages** | **Read strategy:** Short & sharp — aaram se 1 din.

## Kyun padhni hai
Phase 2 shuru. Ye ek clean, no-nonsense guide hai — sochne ko *logical* kaise banaya jaaye. Bias-awareness ke baad, ab reasoning ki structure.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Be attentive** — pehle observe, phir conclude. Clear thinking ki neenv = clear seeing.
- **Truth of premises + validity of form** — dono chahiye. Sahi conclusion ke liye premises bhi sach ho.
- **First principles of logic** — identity, non-contradiction, excluded middle.
- **Efficient communication** — clear language = clear logic.
- **Common logical fallacies** — aur unse kaise bachein.

## Placement application
> Jab team mein koi "claim" ho ("ye company waste of time hai") — poocho: *premise sach hai? conclusion premise se aata hai?* Emotion-driven arguments ko logic se replace karo. Tum "structured" is liye dikhoge kyunki tum form check karte ho.
