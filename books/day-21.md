## Kyun padhni hai
Decision-making ke 4 sabse bade villains, aur unhe harane ka **WRAP** framework. Clean, memorable, immediately usable.

## Key ideas — WRAP (dhyaan idhar)
- **W — Widen your options:** "karun ya na karun" (narrow framing) se bacho — aur options socho. "Aur kya kar sakte hain?"
- **R — Reality-test assumptions:** opposite consider karo; chhote experiments ("ooch") chalao before committing.
- **A — Attain distance:** emotion hatane ke liye **10/10/10** (10 min / 10 months / 10 years baad kaisa lagega?).
- **P — Prepare to be wrong:** pre-mortem + **tripwires** ("agar X ho to plan change").

## Placement application
> Har bade decision pe WRAP: sirf 2 companies nahi — **widen** the list. Assumptions ko chhote pilot se **reality-test** karo. **Tripwire** lagao: "agar Nov tak big-company conversion X% se kam, to strategy revisit."
