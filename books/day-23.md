# Day 23 — Algorithms to Live By — Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths
**Phase 4: Decisions & probability** | **~280 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — mind-expanding, thoda ruk ke socho.

## Kyun padhni hai
Computer science ke algorithms ko human decisions pe apply karna. Ye tumhe surprisingly practical "rules" deta hai everyday choices ke liye.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Optimal stopping (37% rule)** — options mein se ~37% dekho (sirf observe), phir usse behtar pehla le lo.
- **Explore vs Exploit** — naya try karna vs jo pata hai use maximize karna. (Time zyada → explore; kam → exploit.)
- **Sorting & caching** — kya organize karna worth hai; frequently-used cheezein haath ke paas.
- **Bayes rule** — prior + evidence = updated belief.
- **Overfitting** — zyada complex model = future pe kharab. Simpler often better.

## Placement application
> **Explore/exploit:** season ke start mein naye companies explore karo, end ke paas known winners exploit karo. **37% rule:** candidate ya company shortlisting mein pehle survey, phir commit. **Overfitting:** apne process ko itna complicated mat banao ki sirf ek scenario mein chale.
