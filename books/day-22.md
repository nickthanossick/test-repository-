# Day 22 — Fooled by Randomness — Nassim Nicholas Taleb
**Phase 4: Decisions & probability** | **~300 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — opinionated, enjoy the attitude.

## Kyun padhni hai
Luck ko skill samajhna sabse mehanga bias hai. Taleb sikhata hai randomness ko respect karna — taaki tum shor (noise) ko signal na samjho.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Luck vs skill** — successful log akshar lucky hote hain, par khud ko skilled maante hain.
- **Survivorship bias** (deep version) — jo "jeet gaye" sirf wahi dikhte hain.
- **Alternative histories** — jo *ho sakta tha* usse decision judge karo, sirf jo hua usse nahi.
- **Noise vs signal** — zyada data = zyada noise, zaroori nahi behtar.
- **Black swan preview** — rare bade events sab kuch badal dete hain; unke liye margin of safety.

## Placement application
> Jo strategy "chal gayi" — confirm karo wo *skill* thi ya *luck*. Ek quarter ka spike noise ho sakta hai. **Alternative histories:** ek acha decision jo bura nikla, usse punish mat karo (na khud ko, na team ko). Margin of safety rakho — sab candidates ek company pe daant mat lagao.
