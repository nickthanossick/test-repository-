# Day 2 — Thinking in Bets — Annie Duke
**Phase 1: Bias & mind** | **~270 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal.

## Kyun padhni hai
Sabse important mindset shift: **achha decision ≠ achha outcome.** Poker champion + decision scientist ka lens. Ye book pura repo ka DNA hai.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Resulting** — outcome se decision judge karna (galti). Decision *process* judge karo, result nahi.
- **Think in bets** — har decision ek bet hai under uncertainty. "Main kitna sure hoon?" (0–100%).
- **Luck vs skill** — result mein dono ka mix; alag karo.
- **Truthseeking group** — aise log jo aapko galti batayein, sirf haan-mein-haan na milayein.
- **"Wanna bet?"** — apne belief pe paisa lagane ko taiyaar? Nahi → belief kamzor hai.

## Placement application
> Har strategy call ko **bet** ki tarah dekho: "Is company pe effort lagane ka expected value kya hai? Main kitna confident hoon?" Aur team decisions ko **resulting** se mat aank — ek offer miss hua matlab process galat tha, ye zaroori nahi.
