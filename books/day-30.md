# Day 30 — So Good They Can't Ignore You — Cal Newport 🏁
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~270 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — aaram se, ye finale hai. Reflect karo.

## Kyun padhni hai
**Perfect closer.** Iska core thesis is poore repo ka thesis hai: "follow your passion" nahi — **so good bano ki tumhe ignore hi na kiya jaa sake.** Ye 30 din ka nichod hai — skill + rare-and-valuable ability = irreplaceable.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Career Capital Theory** — rare + valuable skills = capital, jise tum great work ke liye "kharch" karte ho.
- **Craftsman mindset > Passion mindset** — "duniya mujhe kya de" nahi, "main duniya ko kya de sakta hoon."
- **Deliberate practice** — apni ability ke edge pe consistent, uncomfortable practice.
- **Control** — asli autonomy career capital se aati hai (pehle skill, phir freedom).
- **Mission** — bada purpose bhi career capital ke baad crystallize hota hai.

## Placement application
> Ye tumhari **"lethal machine"** ki philosophy hai: irreplaceable pad se nahi, *rare + valuable skill + owned systems* se bante ho. 230+ offers, big-company system, structured thinking — ye tumhara **career capital** hai. Isko compound karo. Jab tumhare paas woh skill + system ho jo kisi aur ke paas nahi — tumhare jaane se structure sach mein hilta hai.
