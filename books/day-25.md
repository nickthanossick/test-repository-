# Day 25 — The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People — Stephen Covey ⚠️
**Phase 5: Strategy & leverage** | **~350 pages** | **Read strategy:** Habit 1-3 (private victory) MUST; 4-7 selective.

## Kyun padhni hai
Phase 5 shuru: ab thinking ko *character + effectiveness* mein convert. **Habit 1 ("Be Proactive")** is repo ka theme hai — react vs respond ka classic source.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **1. Be Proactive** — stimulus & response ke beech choose karo. **Circle of Influence vs Circle of Concern** — apni energy influence pe lagao.
- **2. Begin with the End in Mind** — pehle destination clear, phir action.
- **3. Put First Things First** — important vs urgent matrix; important-not-urgent pe invest.
- **4. Think Win-Win** — abundance mindset.
- **5. Seek First to Understand** — empathic listening.
- **6. Synergize** + **7. Sharpen the Saw** (self-renewal).

## Placement application
> **Circle of Influence:** market/companies pe control nahi — apne system, prep, relationships pe hai. Wahan invest karo. **Important-not-urgent:** roz thoda system-building (jo urgent nahi lagta) hi tumhe irreplaceable banata hai. **Win-Win:** team ko upar uthao — abundance, scarcity nahi.
