# Day 5 — You Are Not So Smart — David McRaney
**Phase 1: Bias & mind** | **~300 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — punchy, entertaining.

## Kyun padhni hai
Phase 1 ka closer-catalog: aur zyada self-delusions. Ab tak tum apne dimaag ke default failure modes ka poora naksha bana chuke hoge.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Confabulation** — dimaag reasons *bana* leta hai jo asli nahi hote, par lagte sach hain.
- **Hindsight bias** — "mujhe to pehle se pata tha" (nahi tha).
- **Texas sharpshooter fallacy** — pehle goli, phir target — pattern jahan hai hi nahi.
- **The Dunning-Kruger effect** — kam jaanne wale zyada confident.
- **Confirmation bias** (fir se, kyunki ye sabse ziddी hai).

## Placement application
> **Hindsight bias** se bacho: har offer/miss ke baad likho *pehle se* tumne kya predict kiya tha (decision journal). **Texas sharpshooter:** data mein "jhootha pattern" mat dhoondho — pehle hypothesis, phir data (McKinsey way, Day 16). **Dunning-Kruger:** naye domain (big companies) mein humility rakho, research se cover karo.
