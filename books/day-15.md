# Day 15 — Bulletproof Problem Solving — Charles Conn & Robert McLean
**Phase 3: Problem solving** | **~300 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — 7-step process pe focus, examples skim.

## Kyun padhni hai
Modern McKinsey partners ka complete, repeatable **7-step** problem-solving system. Ye Day 13-14 ko ek full workflow mein jodta hai.

## Key ideas — The 7 steps (dhyaan idhar)
1. **Define** the problem (sharp, specific).
2. **Disaggregate** — logic trees se todo.
3. **Prioritize** — jahan sabse bada leverage (80/20).
4. **Workplan** — kaun, kya, kab.
5. **Analyze** — data + simple heuristics pehle.
6. **Synthesize** — findings ko ek story mein.
7. **Communicate** — pyramid style.

## Placement application
> Kisi bhi bade placement challenge ("big companies mein conversion kaise 2x karein") pe poora 7-step chalao — define → tree → prioritize top 2 levers → workplan → execute → synthesize → present. Ye process khud ek *system* hai jo tum own kar sakte ho.
