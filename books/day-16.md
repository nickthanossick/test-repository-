# Day 16 — The McKinsey Way — Ethan Rasiel
**Phase 3: Problem solving** | **~200 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — fast, anecdote-driven.

## Kyun padhni hai
World ki top consulting firm kaise sochti hai — short, practical. Hypothesis-driven, fact-based thinking ka mindset.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Hypothesis-driven** — pehle ek initial answer (hypothesis) banao, phir usko test karo. (Analysis paralysis se bachta hai.)
- **MECE** (again — kyunki ye core hai).
- **80/20 rule** — 20% effort se 80% result kahan?
- **Don't boil the ocean** — sab kuch analyze mat karo; jo matter kare wahi.
- **Elevator test** — apna solution 30 sec mein samjha sako.
- **Pre-wiring** — meeting se pehle key logon ko brief karo, surprise mat do.

## Placement application
> **Hypothesis-driven:** "Mera guess: big-company conversion referral-stage pe atak raha hai" → phir data se confirm/deny. **Pre-wiring:** important proposals se pehle key stakeholders ko 1-1 brief karo — meeting mein support ready. **80/20:** 6 log, par 20% companies 80% top offers deti hain — wahan focus.
