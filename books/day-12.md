# Day 12 — The Demon-Haunted World — Carl Sagan ⚠️
**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~450 pages** | **Read strategy:** "The Fine Art of Baloney Detection" chapter MUST; baaki key chapters, rest skim.

## Kyun padhni hai
Phase 2 ka grand finale: **skepticism as a tool.** Sagan ka "Baloney Detection Kit" ek lifelong BS-filter hai. Ye tumhe manipulation aur fake claims se bachata hai.

## Key ideas — Baloney Detection Kit (dhyaan idhar)
- **Independent confirmation** — facts ko multiple sources se verify karo.
- **Encourage debate** — apne view ke against argument invite karo.
- **Multiple hypotheses** — ek se zyada explanation banao, phir test.
- **Quantify** — jo naapa jaa sake, wahan feelings se accha judgment.
- **Falsifiability** — agar koi claim kabhi galat *nahi* ho sakti, to wo useless hai.
- **Occam's razor** — simplest explanation pehle.
- Plus: common fallacies ki list (ad hominem, straw man, false dichotomy...).

## Placement application
> Companies/vendors/"gurus" ke tall claims pe kit chalao: "independent proof hai? quantified hai? falsifiable hai?" Aur apne data ko bhi isi se test karo — tumhara system tabhi bharosemand hoga jab tum khud uska skeptic ban jao.
