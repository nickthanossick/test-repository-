## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Inverse thinking, second-order, Occam, Hanlon** — ab tak dekhe, ek chhat ke neeche.
- **Overton window** — kaunse ideas "acceptable" mane jaate hain (aur wo shift ho sakte hain).
- **Local vs global optimum** — chhoti jeet vs badi jeet.
- **Leverage & force multipliers** — kam input, zyada output kahan.
- **Antifragility, luck surface area** — uncertainty ko fayde mein badalna.

## Placement application
> **Local vs global optimum:** chhoti companies se fast offers = local optimum; big-company focus = global optimum (short-term kam, long-term bada). **Luck surface area:** jitna zyada connect + visible ho, utne "lucky" break — referral network expand karo.
