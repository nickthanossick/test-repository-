# Day 3 — The Great Mental Models Vol 1: General Thinking Concepts — Shane Parrish
**Phase 1: Bias & mind** | **~200 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — short, dense-with-value.

## Kyun padhni hai
The foundational "thinking tools." Har model ek lens hai reality ko behtar dekhne ka. Ye tumhare OS ke core models ko deepen karegi.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Map is not the territory** — model reality nahi hai; assumptions kabhi-kabhi galat.
- **Circle of competence** — jaano ki tum *actually* kya jaante ho; boundary respect karo.
- **First principles** — base truths tak todo, phir naya banao.
- **Second-order thinking** — "aur phir kya?"
- **Inversion** + **Occam's razor** + **Hanlon's razor** ("stupidity, not malice").

## Placement application
> **Circle of competence:** un companies/roles pe focus jahan tumhari understanding sabse deep hai — wahan conversion sabse zyada. Baaki ke liye system + research se circle expand karo. **Map ≠ territory:** tumhara tracker ek map hai — real candidate readiness verify karo.
