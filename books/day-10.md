**Phase 2: Logic & argument** | **~350 pages** | **Read strategy:** Part 2 (Analytical Reading) pe focus; Part 3-4 skim. Ironic but true — isko *smartly* padho.

## Kyun padhni hai
Meta-skill: **behtar padhna** = behtar seekhna. Ye book baaki 29 books ko 2x effective bana degi. Isliye ye jaldी rakhi hai.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **4 levels:** Elementary → Inspectional (skim) → Analytical (deep) → Syntopical (many books ek topic pe).
- **Inspectional reading** — systematic skim: contents, index, key paragraphs — 30 min mein book ka "naksha".
- **Come to terms** — author ke key words ka matlab pin karo.
- **X-ray the book** — uska skeleton (main argument) nikaalo.
- **Syntopical reading** — ek topic pe kai books ko aapas mein baat karao (yahi tum is repo mein kar rahe ho!).

## Placement application
> **Inspectional reading** ko roz apply karo — baaki 20 books isse fast honge. **Syntopical:** placement pe alag-alag sources (books, seniors, data) ko ek framework mein merge karo — wahi asli insight deta hai.
