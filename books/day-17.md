# Day 17 — Thinking in Systems — Donella Meadows
**Phase 3: Problem solving** | **~240 pages** | **Read strategy:** Normal — concepts pe ruk-ruk ke socho.

## Kyun padhni hai
Game-changer. Problems ko isolated events ki tarah nahi, **systems** ki tarah dekhna — jahan structure behaviour banati hai. "Lethal machine" playbook ka intellectual base yahi hai.

## Key ideas (dhyaan idhar)
- **Stocks & flows** — kya accumulate hota hai vs kya usko badalta hai.
- **Feedback loops** — reinforcing (snowball) vs balancing (thermostat).
- **Delays** — cause aur effect ke beech time-gap → overreaction ki wajah.
- **Leverage points** — chhoti jagah push karo, bada system change (Meadows ki famous list).
- **Bounded rationality** — log apne limited info mein "rational" hote hain — system unko waisa banati hai.

## Placement application
> Apni team ko ek **system** ki tarah dekho: kaunse feedback loops offers ko badhate/rokte hain? Sabse bada **leverage point** kahan hai (shayad tumhara data-system hi)? Us leverage point ko own karke tum poore system ko control karte ho — individual tasks se nahi, structure se.
