# 01 — The 30-Day / 30-Book Plan

> **Pace:** Roz 1 book (~250 pages). Long books (⚠️) ke liye niche "active skim" strategy di hai — jisse 1 din realistic rahe.
> **Rule:** Consuming nahi, *capturing* karna hai. Har book se 3 models + 1 placement application nikaalo (`02-daily-method.md`).

5 phases hain — har phase ek layer add karta hai. Order maayne rakhta hai, isliye sequence follow karo.

---

*Goal: apne dimaag ke default bugs pehchano.*

| Day | Book | Author | ~Pages | Focus |
|-----|------|--------|--------|-------|
| 1 | The Art of Thinking Clearly | Rolf Dobelli | 340 | 99 short cognitive biases — easy start |
| 2 | Thinking in Bets | Annie Duke | 270 | Decision ≠ outcome; think in probabilities |
| 3 | The Great Mental Models Vol 1 | Shane Parrish | 200 | Map/territory, circle of competence, first principles |

| 5 | You Are Not So Smart | David McRaney | 300 | Self-delusion & bias catalog |

> ⚠️ **Day 6 strategy:** Poori Part 1 + 3 padho; Part 2 (heuristics) ke key chapters; Part 4-5 skim. Notes pe zyada time do.

## 📐 Notes

- **Swap allowed:** koi book na mile ya boring lage — same phase ki koi aur classic swap kar do. Phase ka *goal* maayne rakhta hai, book nahi.
- **Access:** local library, Kindle, Audible (long books audio pe 2x acche lagte hain), ya legit sources use karo.
- **Long-book rule (⚠️):** poora "padhna" zaroori nahi — *understand + capture models* zaroori hai. Skim smart, notes deep.
- **Miss a day?** Us din 100-page "greatest hits" summary padho (book ke key chapters) aur aage badho. Streak todna mat.
